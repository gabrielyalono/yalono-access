# Edge Add-ons listing draft — not submitted

**Name:** Yalono Access

**Short description:** Understand the SharePoint permissions and sharing links visible to your Microsoft 365 account.

**Description:**

Yalono Access brings a clear, read-only view of your selected SharePoint library, folder or file into an Edge side panel. Inspect the people and groups Microsoft returns, see actual role names and confirmed inheritance where available, and understand sharing-link scopes, authorized recipients and expiration information.

Search and filter visible entries, explore folders on demand and export the current checked observation. Optional local snapshots are kept on your device; history and differences are available only when current permissions and comparable coverage can be verified.

Microsoft remains the authority for access. Some details may be unavailable to your account. The extension does not read document bodies, create sharing links, change permissions, monitor a tenant in the background or contain AI.

English and French. Requires a Microsoft 365 work/school account, a dedicated Entra app registration and any consent required by your organization. This development preview has not completed tenant or Edge Store acceptance.

**Category suggestion:** Productivity.

**Permission disclosures:** See SECURITY.md. No analytics, ads, data brokerage or integrated AI. Access metadata can include personal/confidential information and stays local except explicit user exports/copy actions and the delegated Microsoft requests themselves.

**Screenshots to prepare:** owner-style fictional view, sharing links with honest expiration states, folder navigation, unavailable-visibility state. Always label fictional scenarios. Do not upload customer names, live links or tokens.

No Store account enrollment, payment, submission or marketing message has been performed.
